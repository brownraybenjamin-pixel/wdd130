At Rapid River Adventures, we offer exciting white water rafting experiences for everyone. Whether you are a first-time rafter or an experienced adventurer, our professional guides are ready to take you on an unforgettable journey down the river.

Family River Adventure

Enjoy a relaxing and exciting rafting experience designed for families and beginners. This adventure features gentle rapids, beautiful river scenery, and plenty of opportunities to enjoy the outdoors. Our experienced guides provide instructions and help everyone feel comfortable on the water.

Duration: 2 Hours

Difficulty: Easy

Half Day Adventure

Experience the perfect combination of adventure and natural beauty. Our Half Day Adventure takes you through exciting river sections featuring moderate rapids and breathtaking scenery. This trip is ideal for guests looking for more excitement while enjoying a guided outdoor experience.

Duration: 4 Hours

Difficulty: Moderate

Price: $95 per person

Extreme Rapids Expedition

Get ready for an adrenaline-filled adventure through challenging river rapids. This trip is designed for experienced rafters who want to test their skills and enjoy an exciting journey. Our professional guides lead the expedition while emphasizing teamwork, preparation, and safety throughout the trip.

Duration: 6 Hours

Difficulty: Challenging

Price: $145 per person
